More consistent wording.
A narrower claim.

Semantic retrieval recovered useful examples below a fuzzy threshold. The clearest observed benefit was consistency with memory—not established accuracy improvement.

What changed in the audit

The original experiment supplied at least one semantic example even when it recorded route=none. Its full 300-segment result measures that exploratory treatment, not the selective router. The corrected evaluation now calls the same router as the CLI.

85 of 300 candidates passed the similarity floor. Of those, 65 were judged reusable. Passing a threshold and being useful are different outcomes.
