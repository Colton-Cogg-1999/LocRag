2. **0:20–0:45:** Open “Terminology recovered.” Inspect the fuzzy match, semantic candidate, wording changes and reference.
3. **0:45–1:10:** Open “When to decline.” Switch between selective replay and the original experiment. Explain the difference between a research treatment and the deployment policy.
4. **1:10–1:30:** Open “When retrieval hurts.” Show added content copied from memory. Similarity cannot verify meaning.
5. **1:30–1:50:** Open results. Distinguish 85 passing candidates from 65 judged reusable, and preference from accuracy. Show the reproducible audit.
6. **1:50–2:00:** Open the [final policy comparison](selective-router-results.md). Show the below-cutoff consistency and accuracy table, then trace the “Starter pack” example through the router.
